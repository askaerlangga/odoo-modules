Advanced Approval Matrix — Stock Inventory Bridge

Connects the universal approval engine to Physical Inventory Adjustments (stock.quant). Enforce managerial sign-offs before inventory variances and stock ledger movements are committed.

Key Capabilities & Execution Guards

Maintain strict governance and stock valuation integrity across warehouse adjustments.

🛡️

Strict Apply Interception

Intercepts Odoo's native action_apply_inventory(). Any attempt to apply stock differences without final approval clearance is immediately blocked.

🔒

Auto-Apply Bypass Prevention

Prevents accidental bypass from Location view by locking inventory_quantity_auto_apply whenever an approval matrix is active or pending review.

⚙️

Automatic Inventory Posting

When configured with Auto Confirm, stock moves and physical adjustments post automatically the instant the final approver signs off.

🔍

Dedicated Search Filters

Quickly filter quants by approval lifecycle: "Needs Approval", "To My Approval", and "Approved" directly in the Inventory UI.

🧩

Modular Architecture

Keeps the core approval engine completely decoupled and generic. Warehouse teams can install or uninstall this bridge without affecting other models.

🧪

Verified Test Suite

Thoroughly covered with unit tests checking bypass attempts, auto-apply locks, multi-tier clearances, and rollback scenarios.

How It Works

1. Count & Draft

Warehouse users record physical counts. If the difference or quant matches an active approval rule, the record enters "Waiting Approval".

2. Review & Sign-off

Warehouse managers review differences, approve or reject via the systray badge or document action wizard with mandatory audit comments.

3. Ledger Clearance

Upon final approval, the inventory adjustment is cleared and applied, safely booking stock valuation and inventory moves.
